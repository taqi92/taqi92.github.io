About

A player-coach, not a manager who forgot how to ship.

I started as an Android developer in 2017 and never stopped shipping. Along the way I picked up the part of the job most engineers avoid — hiring, sprint facilitation, client discovery, the difficult conversations — and discovered I liked it as much as the code.

At Miaki Media I built a mobile division from scratch for a major telecom VAS platform: hired the team, set up the release pipeline, defined the code review bar. At Dhrubok I led a 4–5 person squad through full product lifecycles — discovery to Play Store — and ran the agile ceremonies as a Certified Scrum Master.

Today at Truely I'm the senior IC and mentor on a remote mobile team scaling a global eSIM platform. I drove the multi-module refactor, owned the CI/CD pipeline, and standardised a reusable Flutter/Bloc skeleton the squad now ships on daily. The platform grew 8× users and 9× revenue across 150+ countries during my time on it.

I'm looking for an Engineering Manager — Mobile role where technical credibility and squad leadership matter equally. Berlin, Amsterdam, remote-EU all welcome.
