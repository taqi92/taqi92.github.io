About

Android first. Still building.

I started as an Android developer in 2017 and never stopped shipping. Flutter came next, then the parts of the job that sit around the code — architecture decisions, sprint facilitation, hiring, mentoring, the difficult conversations.

At Miaki I was the sole mobile engineer for a telecom VAS platform — owned Android development end-to-end, built the release pipeline from scratch, cut crash rates 30%. At Dhrubok I led a 4–5 person squad through full product lifecycles — discovery to Play Store — and ran the agile ceremonies as a Certified Scrum Master.

Most recently at Truely, a global travel eSIM product, I led mobile engineering — hired the team's second engineer, owned sprint planning and architecture, built the CI/CD pipeline that cut release cycles 40%, and bridged the Flutter app into native Android eUICC APIs. The platform grew 8× users and 9× revenue across 150+ countries during my time there (Mar 2025 – Apr 2026).

I'm now expanding deliberately into Kotlin Multiplatform, mobile system design, and Kotlin backend development — building on the Android and Flutter foundation rather than replacing it. I'm a senior mobile engineer who can lead technically, not an engineering manager who happens to code.

Open to relocation across Ireland, New Zealand and Europe, and to strong-fit opportunities elsewhere internationally.
